    About AccessiScroll

    Version — built with determination, passion, and assistive technology.

    In August of 2022, my life changed forever. What began as a beautiful evening in Paris during a business trip ended with a catastrophic spinal cord injury that left me paralysed from the shoulders down. In an instant, I went from walking through the streets of Paris to fighting for my life on a cold bathroom floor, barely able to breathe or call for help.

    What followed was months of survival, surgeries, intensive care, and the long road of rehabilitation. I endured searing pain, trauma, and moments of hopelessness that nearly broke me. But I also discovered something else: the power of the human spirit to endure.

    Technology has always been my passion and I needed to find a way to get back the capability to continue using technology to help me create, explore and innovate.

    I built this entire extension without the use of my hands—using a head-tracking Bluetooth mouse, voice commands, and assistive tech that helps me code, create, and connect. If you want to see my technology set up and how I do this check out my Instagram link below.

    I built this extension because modern websites ship razor-thin, low-contrast scrollbars that are almost impossible to see and grab with a head-tracking mouse. AccessiScroll widens and recolors the scrollbar on every site so it's easy to find and drag, no matter what input device you use. That's all it does. If you're reading this, hopefully it's helping you too.
